📊 Where do customers drop off in a marketing funnel? I analyzed 41,000+ campaign contacts to find out.

For my Future Interns Data Science project (Task 3), I built a funnel and conversion analysis on a bank's telemarketing campaigns (Contacted → Engaged → Interested → Subscribed).

🔎 What I found
• Overall conversion: 11.3% – with the biggest leaks at Engaged → Interested (44% drop) and Interested → Subscribed (77% drop)
• Cellular converts 2.8x better than landline (14.7% vs 5.2%) and needs ~3x fewer calls per sale
• Previously successful customers convert at 65% – 5.8x the average – yet make up only 3% of contacts
• Contacts called 5+ times consumed 38% of all calls but delivered just 6.6% of sales
• Students, retirees and 65+ customers convert at 2–4x average but are under-targeted
• Conversion was ~4x higher when interest rates were low – context matters

✅ What I'd recommend
Prioritise cellular, cap attempts at 3–4, call warm leads first, tailor offers to high-intent segments, and test better scripts + follow-ups to close interested prospects.

🛠 Tools: Python (pandas, Matplotlib), Chart.js, GitHub
💡 Learnings: funnels need clear stage definitions, and correlation ≠ causation – month, year and interest rate were confounded, so I flagged that instead of over-claiming.
