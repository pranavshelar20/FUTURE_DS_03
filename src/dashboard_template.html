Method & caveats

Dataset has no website-traffic or lead stages, so the funnel is built from call engagement. Call duration is only known after the call, so it explains outcomes but cannot be used to pre-select who to call. Month, year and Euribor rate are confounded (2008 was heavy-volume, high-rate; 2009–10 was smaller and targeted), so seasonality should not be read as causal. Year is inferred from the chronological row order. Segment rates are associations, not causal effects.
